FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Does Gatekeeper use the vanilla whitelist?

No. A fresh install denies everyone, unless a whitelist.json exists, in which case Gatekeeper imports it.

Do I need to restart after changing the config?

Usually not. Run /gatekeeper reload.

What happens to a player who is online when I ban them?

They're disconnected right away if their new status doesn't allow them to stay.

Do Bedrock players work?

Yes, through Geyser/Floodgate. See Installation.

How do I change what a denied player sees?

Use the Screen Configurator, then paste the result into your config and reload. Details are on Denial screens.

Who can run commands?

The console, and OP level 3 or higher in game.

Is it safe to expose the API?

Only behind a TLS reverse proxy, with your admin token kept private. See Security.

What if an access check errors?

The player is denied. Gatekeeper fails closed.
